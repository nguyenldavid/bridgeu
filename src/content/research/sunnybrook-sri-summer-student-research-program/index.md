## Eligibility

For all student programs, except the Focused Ultrasound High School Summer Research Program, applicants must be enrolled in a university program (not necessarily at the University of Toronto) and have at least a B+ average in their most recent year of study. Students who have recently completed a program but demonstrate a strong intent to pursue a career in medical research may also be considered. Students holding a visa are eligible only if the visa permits.
