## Eligibility

Research must focus on NeuroCAM.

Applicants need a supervisor/mentor (Assistant, Associate, or Full Professor) from an accredited university.

Open to Canadian or international students working at select universities.

Applicants must be enrolled in an undergraduate program or recently completed one (not started graduate work).

If the research requires ethics clearance, evidence must be provided before funds are released.

The project supervisor must submit a reference survey by the application deadline.

If awarded multiple grants, the Branch Out award will act as a "top-up" award.
