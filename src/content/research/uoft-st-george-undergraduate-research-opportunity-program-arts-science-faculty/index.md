## Eligibility

To be eligible for the ROP, students must be registered as full- or part-time students entering their second or third year of an undergraduate degree program within the Faculty of Arts & Science at the St. George campus. Additionally, they must have accumulated between 4.0-13.5 credits (including transfer credits) by the end of the April exam period.
