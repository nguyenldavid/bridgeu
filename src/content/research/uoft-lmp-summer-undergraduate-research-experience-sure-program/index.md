## Eligibility

To participate in the LMP SURE Program, applicants must have at least a B+ average (cGPA of 3.3) in an undergraduate program, be registered full-time in the academic term immediately prior to the program, and expect to return to full-time studies after the program. Applicants must secure an LMP supervisor before applying and must be a Canadian citizen, permanent resident, or international student with a valid student visa and social insurance number. Students who have completed their undergraduate program by the start of the LMP SURE program (May 12, 2025) are not eligible, unless they are accepted into or enrolled in a professional program (e.g., medical degree) or have been accepted into a graduate program they have not yet started.
