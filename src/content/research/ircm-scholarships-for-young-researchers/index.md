## Eligibility

To be eligible for the IRCM Grants for Young Researchers, applicants must be Canadian citizens, permanent residents, or foreign students with a work permit in Canada. They must be enrolled in a relevant bachelor’s program for at least one year, with a minimum GPA of 3.3/4.3. Applicants must show a strong interest in biomedical research, not have previously received an IRCM scholarship, and secure support from a research director.
