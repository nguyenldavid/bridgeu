## Eligibility

At the time of application, students must be in the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year of an undergraduate degree program, or in medical school. Students enrolled in a Master’s or PhD program are not eligible for summer studentships. Applicants must also be eligible to work in Canada.
