## Eligibility

Domestic students applying to the IMS Summer Undergraduate Research Program must be pursuing an undergraduate degree in science or be in the first or second year of medical school (without a previously obtained graduate degree). Undergraduate applicants must have a minimum B+ cumulative GPA (3.3/4.0 or 77%), while those applying for IMS funding must have a GPA of A- (3.7/4.0 or 80%) or higher. Medical students must be in good standing and not hold a graduate degree. International students currently studying in Canadian universities with a SIN should apply as domestic students. International applicants enrolled at post-secondary institutions outside Canada must have a minimum B+ average (77%) or higher.
