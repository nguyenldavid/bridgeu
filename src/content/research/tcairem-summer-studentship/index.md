## Eligibility

This program is open to students from any Canadian university who are currently enrolled in any undergraduate or professional degree program (e.g., medicine), and we encourage applications from students in all disciplines. Students who will complete an undergraduate or medical degree program in May 2025, or who will be enrolled in a graduate degree program starting in September 2025, are also eligible to apply. International students enrolled in an undergraduate or medical degree program at a Canadian university are eligible if they are legally allowed to work in Canada. Students in course-based Master's degree programs are eligible to apply, provided their course load is more than 60% of their degree.
