## Eligibility

Applicants must be actively enrolled in a bachelor’s, graduate, or professional degree program (e.g., BSc, BME, MSc, MPH, MD, PharmD). Students transitioning between two distinct post-secondary degree programs during the award period are also eligible. Students who are unable to identify a supervisor may be placed on a waiting list.
