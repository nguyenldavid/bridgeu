## Eligibility

To be eligible for this opportunity, applicants must be full-time undergraduate students at the University of Toronto (excluding professional programs), have completed their third year by April 2025, and self-identify as Black or Indigenous. They must plan to re-register for full-time studies in September 2025 and be available for a 12-16 week full-time position between May 1 and August 31, 2025. Additionally, applicants must have a supervising Principal Investigator affiliated with one of EPIC’s partner institutions. Priority is given to those who haven't received an Inspire Summer Studentship or similar awards previously.
