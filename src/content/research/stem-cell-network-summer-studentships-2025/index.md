## Eligibility

Applicants must be enrolled in an undergraduate life sciences program at a Canadian institution, have support from a prospective supervisor for a stem cell and regenerative medicine research project, and meet Tri-Agency funding eligibility. A minimum GPA of 3.5/4.0 (or equivalent) is required, with GPA conversion for institutions not using a 4.0 scale.
