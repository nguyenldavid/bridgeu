## Eligibility

The BioCanRx Indigenous Student Summer Internship Program offers Indigenous undergraduate students in Canada the chance to gain hands-on research or policy experience in cancer-related projects. Eligible students must have arranged a supervisor at a Canadian university for their research project, which may include clinical, social, economic impact research, Indigenous knowledge integration, knowledge translation, or laboratory-based cancer research.
