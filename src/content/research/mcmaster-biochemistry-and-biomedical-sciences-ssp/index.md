## Eligibility

The program is open to Ontario residents enrolled in a STEM postsecondary degree or diploma program who self-identify as Black, Indigenous, or 2SLGBTQIA+. Applicants must have completed at least one semester of their degree program by May of 2025. Students who will have completed their degree requirements by May of 2025 are not eligible to apply.
