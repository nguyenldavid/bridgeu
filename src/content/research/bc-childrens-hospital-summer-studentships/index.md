## Eligibility

The BC Children’s Hospital Research Institute’s Summer Student Research Program is open to undergraduate and medical students. Applicants must contact potential research supervisors and may apply for funding through the BCCHR Summer Studentship or external funding. International students enrolled in Canadian institutions are eligible if they are legally allowed to work in Canada. Students are responsible for securing their own housing in Vancouver during the program. Supervisors may have funding for students, but students may also apply for non-BCCHR awards or volunteer opportunities.
