## Eligibility

The Women’s Health Summer Studentship Award is open to undergraduate students, medical students, and students in course-based graduate programs who are interested in conducting research related to women’s health. Applicants must develop a research project in collaboration with a UHN Women’s Health Program member and submit a detailed proposal, including letters of support and a CV. The research must align with the program’s goals and result in a paper for publication. Applicants cannot hold another summer studentship award concurrently, and funding is available for up to two years, with a maximum award duration of two years per individual.
