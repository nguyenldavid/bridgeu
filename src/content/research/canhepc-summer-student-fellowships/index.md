## Eligibility

PhD students and post-doctoral trainees must commit at least two years to the program and not be beyond their third year of training by September 1, 2024. MSc and PhD trainees must be full-time students throughout the program. Applicants must be registered or intend to register in a recognized graduate program during the application year. Applicants must be supervised or co-supervised by a CanHepC mentor, with each mentor accepting a maximum of two trainees (three under exceptional circumstances). Mentors must be PIs with a CanHepC CIHR-funded grant and an academic appointment to supervise trainees. Trainees must participate in various program activities. Consult the Award Guide before applying.
