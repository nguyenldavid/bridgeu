## Eligibility

To be eligible for WCHRI funding, applicants must be undergraduate students, including those in medical education, enrolled in a full-time degree program at the University of Alberta. They must commit to a three- or four-month research period between May and August and adhere to University of Alberta policies, including COVID-19 guidelines. The research must be supervised by a WCHRI academic member, who must have held this position for at least one year unless they are a new recruit with an academic appointment under one year. Co-supervision is allowed if the co-supervisor is a current WCHRI Postdoctoral Fellow, and the research directly supports their project. Applicants and supervisors may only submit one application each. Eligibility is confirmed upon submission of the completed application forms.
