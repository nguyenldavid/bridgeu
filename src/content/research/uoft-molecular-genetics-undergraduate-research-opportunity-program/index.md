## Eligibility

Applicants must have at least a B+ average in the two terms of undergraduate studies immediately preceding acceptance of the award. They must have successfully completed a term in a full-time undergraduate program prior to the award period. Students who have completed their undergraduate program are not eligible unless they have been accepted into an undergraduate professional program (e.g., medical degree) or a graduate program they have not yet started. Students currently enrolled in an undergraduate professional program are eligible to apply. Both University of Toronto and non-University of Toronto students, including international students, may apply. Students may submit only one application, and supervisors can be named as the primary supervisor for only one application. Violations of these rules may result in the application being given lower priority or denied.
