## Eligibility

To be eligible for the USRP award, students must be enrolled as full or part-time undergraduates at a Canadian academic institution at the time of application. Up to two awards are reserved for Indigenous students. Applicants must secure two faculty supervisors within the TRANSFORM HF network, one of whom must be affiliated with the Faculty of Applied Science and Engineering (FASE). Students can either consult a list of projects seeking undergraduates and contact the project leads, or directly reach out to faculty members within the TRANSFORM HF network, with FASE faculty highlighted in yellow.
