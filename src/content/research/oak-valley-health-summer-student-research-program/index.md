## Eligibility

Applicants must be entering or completing their final year of undergraduate studies, preferably in a health-related discipline, with plans to return to school in the fall for medical school or a post-graduate degree. They should have excellent computer skills, including Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook, and the ability to work independently in a fast-paced environment while managing multiple deadlines. Knowledge of conducting literature reviews, research methodology, the Research Ethics Board, good clinical practices, and basic data analysis is preferred, with the latter considered an asset.
