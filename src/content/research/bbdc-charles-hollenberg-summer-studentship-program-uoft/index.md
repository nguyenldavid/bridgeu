## Eligibility

The potential supervisor must hold a faculty appointment at the University of Toronto at the assistant, associate, or full professor level and be conducting diabetes research at the time of application. They must also be a registered member of the BBDC; new diabetes researchers can request free membership by contacting the Centre. Supervisors may submit a maximum of two applications as primary and/or co-supervisor, but typically only one studentship will be awarded per supervisor. Applicants must be enrolled in an undergraduate or medical degree program at a Canadian university. International students in these programs are eligible if they are legally permitted to work in Canada, but students from universities outside Canada are not eligible. Students may submit only one application.
