## Eligibility

Eligible students include those enrolled in a full-time undergraduate degree program, full-time medical students, and high school graduates enrolled in a relevant undergraduate program for the fall (proof of enrollment required). International students are eligible if they can legally work in Canada. Students must commit to full-time participation (37.5 hours per week, May to August) unless otherwise approved by their supervisor. A criminal record check is mandatory, but having a criminal record does not automatically disqualify applicants. Ineligible students include high school students, graduate students (http://m.sc/. or Ph.D.), participants in other specific summer student programs, and summer co-op students.
