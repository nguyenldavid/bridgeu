## Eligibility

Any student who will be enrolled in an undergraduate university/college program or medical school in the fall following the Summer Program is eligible to be hired as a Research Summer Student, including high school students in their last year. Students in their final year of undergraduate studies are also eligible as long as they will be, or have applied to be, enrolled in a university/college program in the fall.
