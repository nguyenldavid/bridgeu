## Eligibility

To be eligible, applicants must be full-time students enrolled in an undergraduate, postgraduate, or health professional degree program, either in clinical or non-clinical fields such as health sciences, epidemiology, biostatistics, public health, or medical studies. Candidates should demonstrate a clear interest in pursuing a career in brain health and stroke prevention. They must also be available to work full-time for up to a 12-week period. Additionally, the proposed research project should align with the focus areas of the JSS Centre.
