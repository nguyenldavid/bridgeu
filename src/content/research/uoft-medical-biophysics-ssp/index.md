## Eligibility

The Summer Student program is open to all university undergraduate students in their first, second, or third year of study, as well as co-op students in their 4th year (out of 5). Applicants must have a strong academic record and demonstrated interest in scientific research, with a minimum B+ average for all completed university courses. Applicants must also be legally entitled to work in Canada.
