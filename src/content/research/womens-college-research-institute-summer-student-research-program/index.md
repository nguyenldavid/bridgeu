## Eligibility

To be eligible for the Summer Student Research Program, post-secondary students must be enrolled in an undergraduate, graduate, medical, or health professional program, or be graduating in Spring 2025. High school students must currently be enrolled. The program, in partnership with the Emily Stowe Scholar Program, prioritizes supporting individuals who self-identify as Black, Indigenous, racialized women, 2SLGBTQI+, trans, two-spirit, non-binary, or persons with a disability, particularly those who have been historically excluded from health care and health science careers. Funding is available for individuals from these groups.
