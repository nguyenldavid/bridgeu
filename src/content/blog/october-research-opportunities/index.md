**Welcome (or welcome back) to the BridgeU newsletter!**

Every month, we share the latest research opportunities across Canada with our subscribers. This edition highlights **high-impact summer research opportunities due in October–November 2025**.

* * *

## 📅 Opportunities Due in October–November

### 🎓 **Stantec Equity & Diversity Scholarship**

**For:** Students from underrepresented groups pursuing degrees in Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics (STEAM)

**Deadline:** October 15, 2025

Supports underrepresented students in STEAM fields with scholarships to advance their education.

🔗 [Learn more](https://www.notion.so/webstraw/Blog-0835109c5022490b8cc9320e4941f846?p=31573195f07c8097845dcc4d06752156&pm=c#)

* * *

### 🏆 **Undergraduate Science Case Competition (USCC)**

**For:** All undergraduate students in North America (Lower & Upper Divisions)

**Location:** Ottawa, Ontario, Canada

**Deadline:** October 31, 2025

A competitive case-based event that provides undergraduates with hands-on experience in solving real-world science challenges.

🔗 [Learn more](https://www.notion.so/webstraw/Blog-0835109c5022490b8cc9320e4941f846?p=31573195f07c8097845dcc4d06752156&pm=c#)

* * *

### 💼 **Pathways to Equity Bursary – Toronto Metropolitan University (TMU)**

**For:** Equity-deserving TMU undergraduates in work-integrated learning programs

**Location:** Toronto, Ontario, Canada

**Deadline:** October 31, 2025

Provides bursaries to support underrepresented students pursuing experiential learning opportunities at TMU.

🔗 [Learn more](https://www.notion.so/webstraw/Blog-0835109c5022490b8cc9320e4941f846?p=31573195f07c8097845dcc4d06752156&pm=c#)

* * *

### 🧪 **March Break Internship Program – SciHigh**

**For:** Grade 11 & 12 students interested in hands-on biomedical lab research

**Location:** Toronto, Ontario, Canada

**Applications Open:** November 2025

Offers high school students a chance to gain practical laboratory experience in biomedical sciences.

🔗 [Learn more](https://www.notion.so/webstraw/Blog-0835109c5022490b8cc9320e4941f846?p=31573195f07c8097845dcc4d06752156&pm=c#)
