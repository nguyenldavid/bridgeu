## 🌟 Featured Research Opportunities Due in February

Here’s a round-up of 9 amazing summer research studentships that have application deadlines this February:

### 🔬 **1\. AMGEN Scholars Program** _(Due Feb 1)_

A prestigious summer research opportunity for undergraduates in biomedical sciences.

🔗 [https://amgenscholars.com/university/university-of-toronto/](https://amgenscholars.com/university/university-of-toronto/)

### 🧪 **4\. Baycrest Rotman Research Institute Summer Student Program** _(Mid-late Feb)_

Offers research experience in neuroscience and aging.

Opportunities in physiology research for undergraduate students.

🔗 https://physiology.utoronto.ca/student-research-awards
