**Position description:**

- Girls Ed volunteers work with our participant girls at our After School Program at St. Matthew's Church Hall. We welcome the support of teenaged girls and women of all ages - this is a great way for high school students to get some experience and obtain their 40 service hours!
- If you are interested in connecting with us to learn more about volunteering in any of the above areas, please [click this link and complete our volunteering survey.](https://girlsinclimestone.formstack.com/forms/volunteering_with_girls_inc)
