**Position description:**

- KidsInclusive recognizes that many young adults appreciate the opportunity to observe the unique work that our clinicians do at our centre when planning out their career paths.
- While we strive to provide a number of shadow opportunities, not all requests can be accommodated.
- Please contact the Manager of Clinical Services at 613 544 3400 ext. 3118 to inquire about a shadow opportunity.
- **Please note due to ongoing COVID restrictions we are unable to offer shadowing opportunities at this time.**
