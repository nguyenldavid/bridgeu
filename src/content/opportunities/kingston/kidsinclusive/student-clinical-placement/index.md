**Position description:**

- An important part of our mission at KidsInclusive and Hotel Dieu Hospital is to provide excellent client care while educating the next generation of health care professionals.
- Hotel Dieu Hospital is a teaching hospital for Queen’s University and is affiliated with St. Lawrence College as well as other educational institutions.
- Typically the academic institution contacts the hospital or KidsInclusive directly to request student placements required for course completion.
