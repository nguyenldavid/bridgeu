**Position description:**

- Community members can help newcomer refugee families get settled in Kingston. We are looking for eager and committed volunteers to join Family support teams, lead Family support teams, or do informal language interpretation.  Volunteering with refugee families in our community is a special and rewarding opportunity!
- **Please complete the form** [**here**](https://keys.ca/support/volunteer_form.php) **and we will contact you with next steps.**
