**Position description:**

- Rise is the only national organization providing people with mental health or addiction challenges a path toward sustained self-employment or small business ownership. KEYS Job Centre offers the Rise program in the Kingston area. Mentors provide one-on-one business advice to Rise clients in-person, by phone, and by email. Their support helps end stigma and change lives. **To apply or for more information** [**Click here**](https://risehelps.ca/become-a-volunteer/) **or email** [**rise@keys.ca**](mailto:rise@keys.ca) **or call 613.546.5559**
