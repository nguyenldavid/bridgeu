**Position description:**

- Through this program, Kingston area professionals connect with and support newcomer professionals with similar skills and education through mentorship and networking. Mentors open doors to the realities of their sector and Kingston-area workplaces for newcomers and help them build their social capital and networks. Volunteer opportunities include one-to-one matches, one-time meetings and participation in sector-specific networking events. **To apply or for more information,** [**Click here**](https://keys.ca/immigrants/mentoringservice.php) **or email** [**mentoring@keys.ca**](mailto:mentoring@keys.ca) **or call 613.546.5559**
