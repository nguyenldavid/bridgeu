**Position description:**

# We have a role for you!

We will match your interests and availability to a volunteer role that’s right for you. We offer the training you need and will be in frequent communication. Support is always a call away. Here are a few examples of how you can make a difference as a VON volunteer.

### **Client and Family Advisors**

Your feedback and input will help make VON the best it can be.

### **Transportation**

Drive clients to medical appointments, the grocery store or social activities and programs.

### **Volunteer Visiting**

Brighten the day of an isolated senior, physically disabled adult or person with Alzheimer’s or dementia. Also offered virtually!

### **Security Checks/Reassurance Calls**

Provide a supportive and uplifting daily phone call to make sure our clients are safe and healthy.

### **Adult Day Program**

Assist VON staff in organizing and delivering fun and engaging activities.

### **Home Maintenance**

Help clients with minor home repairs to keep their homes safe.

### **Office Administration**

Support your local VON office with filing, organizing supply rooms or other administrative tasks.

### **Meals on Wheels**

Deliver hot or frozen meals in the community. You may also take clients' meal orders via phone or help to pack meals for delivery.
