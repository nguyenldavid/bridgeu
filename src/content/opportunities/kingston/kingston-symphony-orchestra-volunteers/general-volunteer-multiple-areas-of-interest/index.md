**Position description:**

- Volunteers play a vital role in supporting the Kingston Symphony Association. We are always looking for enthusiastic and dedicated volunteers who are willing to help us reach our goals. Whether you are a student looking for volunteer hours, a lover of classical music or are just looking to give back to the community, we have a position for you!
- Please fill in the following form to become a member of the Kingston Symphony Volunteers. If you are a donor to the Kingston Symphony Association you can join the Volunteers for free. Otherwise, a donation of $20 or more to the Kingston Symphony Association is all that is required. A tax receipt will be provided.
- For general information on how to volunteer, how to join the Kingston Symphony Volunteers or to be notified when volunteer opportunities come up, please contact the Kingston Symphony Association office at 613-546-9729 or email volunteercommittee@kingstonsymphony.ca.
