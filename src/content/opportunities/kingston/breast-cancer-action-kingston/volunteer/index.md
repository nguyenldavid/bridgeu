**Position description:**

- Help outside organizations with fundraising for BCAK.
- Assist at BCAK events or fundraisers.
- Spread awareness of our organization in the community.
- Steps:  
  1\. Complete the application form, [download form](https://www.bcakingston.ca/_files/ugd/adec7a_93d987eb467048f0a3484491668850d3.pdf). Call us at 613-531-7912 or email [admin@bcakingston.ca](mailto:admin@bcakingston.ca) or complete the online Volunteer Form below  
  2\. Our volunteer coordinator will contact you directly to discuss your interests.  
  3\. We welcome all active BCAK members on our volunteer committees.
