**Position description:**

Ryandale is always happy to welcome new board members. If you believe that every person, regardless of past life experience, deserves to be treated with respect, and that housing is fundamental to this, please forward your resume and a cover letter to:

[ryandaleshelter@cogeco.net](mailto:ryandaleshelter@cogeco.net)

with the subject line “Board Membership”. Individuals should ideally have some understanding of the issues facing Kingston’s vulnerable populations, and be willing to contribute in an advisory capacity.

**Time commitment:**

One evening per month, plus some occasional weekend hours.
