**Position description:**

- **Benefits of becoming a YMCA Volunteer**  
  Volunteering isn’t just about giving your time and talent; you get so much back in return. You will:  
  • Give back to your community  
  • Gain valuable experience  
  • Meet people who share your interests  
  • Enhance your leadership skills  
  • Share your knowledge and life lessons with members of your community  
  If you are interested in volunteering, please complete our [Volunteer Application Form](https://eo.ymca.ca/photos/custom/Get%20Involved/Volunteer-Application-Form-2022-1.pdf).  
  For more information, please contact us.
