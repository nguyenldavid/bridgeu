**Position description:**

Enjoy the market during summer while collecting fresh healthy produce at the [Downtown Kingston Public Market](http://kingstonpublicmarket.ca/) or at the [Memorial Centre Farmers Market](https://www.memorialcentrefarmersmarket.ca/) for our [Grow A Row](https://www.lovingspoonful.org/grow-a-row) program.

_Volunteer opportunities are available while markets are open from May to October._
