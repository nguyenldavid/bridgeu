**Position description:**

Help out in our [Community Kitchens](https://www.lovingspoonful.org/community-kitchens) cooking classes where we make healthy, affordable meals, building food literacy and community as we work together in the kitchen and share meals. Some food skills and an outgoing and encouraging attitude are required.

_Volunteer opportunities vary by program need throughout the year._
