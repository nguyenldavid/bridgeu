**Position description:**

Spend time in the fresh air during the summer and fall! Gardeners help to build and establish new gardens to grow more food in the City. [Gleaners](https://www.lovingspoonful.org/gleaning) collect surplus crops from farmers' fields and urban gardens to help reduce food waste and redistribute it to Kingston communities who need it.

_Volunteer opportunities are available from July to November._

Email [Ayla@lovingspoonful.org](mailto:Ayla@lovingspoonful.org) directly for more information or to sign up for Gardening and/or Gleaning.
