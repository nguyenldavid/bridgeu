**Position description:**

- From time to time puppy raisers will need some relief. Whether it is for a weekend when the puppy can not participate, or an unexpected family situation, our dedicated puppy raisers need backup. Volunteers must have experience with dogs. Experience with a Puppy Raising Program is desirable but not essential.

# Volunteer Recognition

K4PSD recognizes the outstanding contributions of our volunteers at our Service Dog graduations and other events.

# Information

To become part of our amazing team of volunteers please complete our Online Volunteer Application, or contact us at [info@k4paws.ca](mailto:info@k4paws.ca)

If you have a couple of hours once a week, once every two weeks or once in a while, we can use your help.
