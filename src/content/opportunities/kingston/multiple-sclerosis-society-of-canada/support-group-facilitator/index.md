Peer Support Groups bring together people living with MS, and family, friends, and caregivers affected by MS, to share common concerns and experiences in an informal and safe environment. We are looking for volunteers to facilitate groups in Alberta, New Brunswick, Ontario, and Saskatchewan. Meeting times and locations vary, with some groups meeting virtually.
