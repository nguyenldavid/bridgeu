The 1:1 Peer Support Program is a telephone / email program where trained volunteers are matched with people who need to connect with someone who has 'been there'. We are currently seeking volunteers who are a loved one of someone with MS to support other caregivers looking for support. Visit https://mssociety.ca/peersupport to learn more or click below to apply!
