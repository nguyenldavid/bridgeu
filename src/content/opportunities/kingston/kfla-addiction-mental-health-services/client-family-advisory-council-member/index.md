**Position description:**

- Members of the CFAC will meet regularly to discuss issues, help advise on matters of policy, program implementation, and more.
- Those 16+ who have accessed services within the past two years, or who have supported a family member or other loved one in their journey to recovery with AMHS-KFLA are welcomed to apply for a position on the council.
