**Position description:**

- We are always looking for volunteers to help out with KAC programs, events, workshops, fundraising, and more. KAC volunteers will have access to all of KAC’s services, including an annual Volunteer Appreciation Arts Social where we celebrate and recognize our volunteers.
- Additionally, we work with our volunteers by acting as a conduit for other volunteer opportunities in the arts in Kingston. The KAC is a network and connected to individuals, collectives, and organizations in the visual arts, film, music, community arts, performing arts, urban arts, literary arts, and much more. As a KAC volunteer, we build you a profile for our [YGK Arts Project](https://www.instagram.com/kingstonartscouncil/) and introduce you to other volunteer opportunities in the arts in Kingston based on your interests and professional development goals.
- If you’d like to gain experience in the arts in Kingston and give back to your community, please contact us at [info@artskingston.ca](mailto:info@artskingston.ca).
