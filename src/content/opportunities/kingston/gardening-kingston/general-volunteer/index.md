**Position description:**

Gardening Kingston is a group of friendly green thumbs. The scope of our activities is limited only by the number of willing volunteers to join in and lend a hand.

Every year, members volunteer thousands of hours running the society, organizing events and other community activities that promote horticulture, community beautification, and protecting the environment. While not a requirement to be a member, we’re always grateful for volunteers to assist with Society activities, including:

- Community gardening at our [Pollinator Garden](https://kingstonhort.ca/pollinator-garden/) and [Paterson Memorial Garden](https://kingstonhort.ca/paterson-garden/)
- [Plant sales](https://kingstonhort.ca/plant-sale/)
- [Garden tours](https://kingstonhort.ca/garden-tours/)
- Serving on the [executive and board](https://kingstonhort.ca/executive-board/), and working on related committees

To volunteer, email us at [kingstonhortsoc@gmail.com](mailto:kingstonhortsoc@gmail.com), or use our online [Contact Form](https://kingstonhort.ca/contact/).
