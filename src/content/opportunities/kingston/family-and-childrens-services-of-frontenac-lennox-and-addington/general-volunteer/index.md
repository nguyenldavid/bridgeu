**Position description:**

### EVERYONE CAN CONTRIBUTE

Our volunteers are men and women, young and old, single and partnered, students, working people, and retirees. They come from diverse cultural, racial, ethnic, and religious groups.

There are a variety of volunteer positions with our agency. Some volunteers work directly with children and families by becoming special friends or tutors. Others participate in children’s groups or take part in ongoing programs that benefit both children and their parents. Some help transport children where they need to go. Some serve on our Board of Directors or help with our fundraising activities. Here are some of the possible volunteer roles available:

- Driver
- Special Friend
- Tutor
- Life Book Creator
- Board Member
- Event and Fundraising Support

Contact us to become a volunteer:

[https://www.facsfla.ca/contact-us/](https://www.facsfla.ca/contact-us/)
