**Position description:**

[](https://www.kingstonpolice.ca/en/careers-and-volunteer/resources/KPCV-sm.jpg)

If you’re interested in applying to become a member of the KPCV, please complete the application form at the link below and either mail it to us or drop it off in person at the front desk of Kingston Police Headquarters, [**705 Division Street**](https://goo.gl/maps/AkB24avQfN42).  Applicants must also complete the Kingston Police Application for Police Check attached to the KPCV Application file.

Applications must be submitted by the 30th of September each year, for we take on new volunteers only once a year.

[https://www.kingstonpolice.ca/en/careers-and-volunteer/resources/KPCV-Application.pdf](https://www.kingstonpolice.ca/en/careers-and-volunteer/resources/KPCV-Application.pdf)
