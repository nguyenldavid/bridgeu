**Position description:**

### **Role: Engaged Seminars Coordinator**

**Description:** Organize summer Waves, our flagship service: diverse, live workshops and seminars that help students continue learning during the summer and discover new passions. Work with instructors, students, and parents to create a fun and fulfilling experience.

**Responsibilities:** Design and review instructor applications, conduct instructor interviews, work closely with instructors to review course material, moderate courses for student safety, collect and analyze student feedback, etc.

**Eligibility:** Junior in high school or above. College students, grad students, and beyond are highly encouraged to apply. Excellent communication skills, organized, detail-oriented. Check out our [past waves](https://www.wavelf.org/courses-archive) to get an understanding of what courses look like!

**Nice to have:** Experience working with students (teaching or tutoring). Leadership experience. Experience coordinating clubs, events, or conferences.

**Time commitment:** 6-8 hours/week

### **Role: Curricular Support Coordinator**

**Description:** Organize our 24/7 tutoring program to support all students in achieving academic excellence. Work with students and mentors to create the optimal learning environment.

**Responsibilities:** Design and review tutor applications, conduct tutor interviews, onboard and support tutors throughout the school year, collect and analyze student feedback, design program improvements, etc.

**Eligibility:** Junior in high school or above. College students, grad students, and beyond are highly encouraged to apply. Excellent communication skills, organized, detail-oriented.

**Nice to have:** Experience working with students (teaching or tutoring). Leadership experience.

**Time commitment:** 5-7 hours/week

### **Role: Special Events Coordinator**

**Description:** Work with speakers and external organizations to put on special events.

**Responsibilities:** Source and collaborate with potential speakers to prepare them for the event, etc.

**Eligibility:** Junior in high school or above. Excellent communication skills, organized, detail-oriented, diligent.

**Nice to have:** Experience organizing events, experience with logistics management, emailing skills.

**Time commitment:** 3-5 hours/week
