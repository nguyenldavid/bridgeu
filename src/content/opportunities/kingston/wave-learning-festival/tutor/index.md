**Position description:**

Do you want to support students learning around the world? Are you excited by the idea of using your expertise in science, math, languages, or the humanities to mentor others? Apply to be a **Wave Tutor**! We are looking for dedicated and passionate volunteers to help middle and high schoolers with all academic subjects.

Eligibility:

To support our focus of "students helping students", you must be

- At least a high school junior
- Currently enrolled in school (e.g. undergraduate, graduate school) or, a recent graduate

Select any variety of subjects you’re comfortable tutoring, and set your own weekly schedule. For as little as an hour a week, you can support another student’s academic journey.

Got questions? Email us at [tutoring@wavelf.org](mailto:%20tutoring@wavelf.org) or check out our FAQs page for tutors [here](https://www.wavelf.org/faq-tutors)!
