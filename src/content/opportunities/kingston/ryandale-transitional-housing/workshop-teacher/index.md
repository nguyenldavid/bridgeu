**Position description:**

- Are you a natural-born teacher with a desire to share a specialized skill? Ryandale is searching for expressions of interest from individuals to present occasional workshops to our residents on topics such as health, nutrition, cooking on a budget, arts and crafts, woodworking, pottery, computer literacy, money management and wellness. Individuals are invited to send a resumé and a cover letter outlining your area of interest to [ryandaleshelter@cogeco.net](mailto:ryandaleshelter@cogeco.net)  
  , with the subject line “Volunteer Opportunities.”
