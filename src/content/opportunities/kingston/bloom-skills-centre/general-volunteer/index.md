**Position description:**

We are always looking for volunteers to support our organization. There is also plenty of ways that you can help. We utilize our volunteers for program support, fundraising, special events, and much more. This is a great opportunity for those looking to fulfill high school volunteer hour requirements.

**Requirements:**

~Volunteers over the age of 18 require a Vulnerable Sector check.

~Volunteers currently enrolled in secondary school require a teacher letter of recommendation.

If you are interested in lending a hand, please contact us in the link below.
