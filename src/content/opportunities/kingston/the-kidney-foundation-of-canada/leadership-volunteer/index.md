**Position description:**

- We are looking for Chapter Leadership Volunteers who will be responsible for assisting the Chapter reach its goals.
- **Requirements:** Works collaboratively with Chapter and or Branch staff; works on activities which align  
  with the Foundation’s strategic plan and supporting Chapters’ development; assist with fundraising  
  campaigns; participates in external activities representing the Foundation; meets regularly with Chapter and Committee Members; undertakes specific assignments.
- **Commitment:** We require up to 1 year of your valuable time attending annual meetings (calendar  
  provided) as well as conference calls if required. We are looking for Community Outreach Volunteers who will be responsible for assisting the Branch reach its goals.
