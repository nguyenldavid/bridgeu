**Position description:**

- **Requirements**: A personal Twitter, Facebook, and/or Instagram account; Email or phone access to work with Chapter and/or Branch staff; Availability to promote fundraising campaigns during Kidney Health Month; Available to meet regularly with Chapter and Committee Members to discuss social media and community outreach.
- **Commitment**: We require up to 1 year of your valuable time.
