**Position description:**

- Operate, interpret, maintain, and improve the model electric railway layouts located in the museum Train Room.

### **CONTACT US ABOUT VOLUNTEERING**

Please send us an email through our [Contact Us](https://www.kingstonpumphouse.ca/contact) page, call us at 613-544-7867, or visit the front desk during opening hours to inquire about volunteer opportunities at the Pumphouse.

### **APPLYING TO VOLUNTEER**

Once we learn that you are interested in volunteering and staff have had a chance to speak with you about your potential role, we will give you an application package, which must be completed and returned for volunteer consideration.

Once your completed volunteer application package is recieved it will be reviewed by the appropriate staff member who will then contact you to let you know if your application has been approved. After approval your volunteer registration/sign-on will take place. This process will include:

- Police Background Check (CPIC) - Provided your CPIC is clear and your application to volunteer is successful we will reimburse you the cost for this check. Please note, we require that all CIPC's include vulnerable sector screening.
- Review and sign-off of volunteer handbook
- Training as necessary
