**Position description:**

- This team supports our wonderful instructors during their classes by providing 1:1 support, assisting with studio set-up and clean-up, and documenting classes. These positions are often filled by Practicum Students from Queen’s University and St. Lawrence College. A CPIC is required to work with our Youth Programming.
