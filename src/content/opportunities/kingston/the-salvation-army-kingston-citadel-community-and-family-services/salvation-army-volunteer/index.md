**Position description:**

- What volunteers have in common is the desire to make a difference in their community – and in their own life – by giving of their time and expertise.
- When you volunteer you are giving something back to your community by lending a helping hand to people, businesses and organizations. What you may not realize is that volunteering also benefits you as an individual. There are many good reasons to get involved in your community and the Salvation Army offers volunteer opportunities for everyone regardless of age.
- Community and Family Services endeavours to provide volunteer opportunities that effectively incorporate each person’s individual skills, interests and time commitments including:  
  • Administrative Support  
  • Community Choice Pantry  
  • Community Garden  
  • Stocking Shelves  
  • Christmas Hampers  
  • Christmas Kettles  
  • Pure Country 99 and Move 98.3's Toy Mountain  
  • Santa Shuffle  
  • Volunteer Income Tax Clinic

If you would like to volunteer please fill out the form and email it to [receptionist@kingstonsa.ca](mailto:receptionist@kingstonsa.ca).
