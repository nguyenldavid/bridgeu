**Position description:**

• Volunteers must be **16 years of age** or older to volunteer in the warehouse. We are accepting a limited number of students at this time.  
• Please check your email and spam filters prior to a volunteer shift to ensure you are receiving registration, reminder emails and volunteer news from our system.Our volunteers provide essential services in many ways:  
• Assemble daily hampers  
• Sort food donations  
• Breakdown and portion bulk items  
• Keep the warehouse clean and safe
