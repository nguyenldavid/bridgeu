**Position description:**

- Programming Volunteers assist in the delivery of educational programming by supporting the work of the Beyond Classrooms Coordinator, the classroom teacher and host site staff, during program weeks.
- Generally this would involve serving as an adult mentor, working with a small group of students as they explore the host site, and participate in scheduled activities such as journaling and sketching.
- In special cases, Programming Volunteers may also be engaged short term projects related to the development of thematic and/or curriculum linked programming.
- All Programming Volunteers are required to provide current CPIC documentation.
