**Position description:**

- Marketing and Promotion volunteers assist with the implementation of Beyond Classrooms media and communications strategy by taking photographs and video footage, and writing social media posts and media releases in support of program week activity.
- In special cases, Marketing and Promotion volunteers may also be engaged in short term projects related to the development of promotional tools.
- Marketing and Promotion Volunteers are required to provide current CPIC documentation.
