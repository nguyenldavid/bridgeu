**Position description:**

- Most of the apparel we provide to our clients comes to us from other members of the community, and hosting a donation drive is a great way to support Well Suited Kingston.
- Your donation drive can collect new or nearly-new suits, other business-appropriate apparel, and shoes.
- For more information on hosting a donation drive please contact us by email at wellsuitedkingston@gmail.com.
