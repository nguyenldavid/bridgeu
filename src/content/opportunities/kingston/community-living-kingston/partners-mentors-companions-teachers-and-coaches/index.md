**Position description:**

- There are a number of possibilities for you to share your time, energy and talents. We need volunteers to act as recreational partners, mentors, companions, teachers and coaches. Opportunities exist to provide assistance to people of all ages and abilities and in a number of settings.
- If you are interested in volunteering, please fill out the [Volunteer Application Form](https://myclkd.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Volunteer-Application-2019.doc) and send it to Eleonor Egidio, Volunteer Services Coordinator by [email](mailto:volunteer@clkingston.ca) or call [613.546.6613](http://613.546.6613/) ext. 2132.
