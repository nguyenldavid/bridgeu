**Position description:**

- **Walking Temperamental Dogs -** Will need to be an experienced dog walker to apply.  Dog walkers will complete onsite training, once training is complete, they will be informed if they qualify for the program. For more information please contact our volunteer coordinator at [volunteer@kingstonhumanesociety.ca](mailto:volunteer@kingstonhumanesociety.ca)
