**Position description:**

- **Wellness Support:** volunteers support clients on Wednesday and Thursday afternoons as they participate in a facilitated support group segment, followed by a therapeutic activity like music therapy, arts and crafts, seated yoga and one-to-one time with volunteers. Volunteers participate in the group’s activities, provide support to individual clients as needed.
