**Position description:**

- **Walking Group Leaders:** _(Currently supended)_ volunteers lead the bereavement walking group (Wednesday mornings, spring through fall, at Lemoine Conservation Point Area), attended by bereavement services clients. The walking group leaders welcome participants, lead the walk, and provide conversation and support as needed.
