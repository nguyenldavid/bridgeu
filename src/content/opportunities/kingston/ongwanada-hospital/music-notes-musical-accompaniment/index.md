**Position description:**

Share your musical talents, whether it be an angelic voice or the playing of an instrument. Share those sweet sounds to compliment memorial services, Christmas time caroling, Spiritual services, our Circle of a Friends music club, and other special Ongwanada events.

**Commitment = varies – can be occasional, on-call, and/or once per week for consistently scheduled groups/services.**
