**Position description:**

Be a buddy, be a friend!! In Kingston, this program operates through Best Buddies Canada, Ongwanada, Community Living Kingston, Queen’s University & St. Lawrence College. Students enrolled at either Kingston Post-Secondary School can apply to become a Student Buddy and will be matched/introduced to an individual supported by either agency. Buddy/friend pairs have a blast enjoying community outings together and attending the more significant group-themed events.

**Commitment = September to April (school year), minimum twice monthly.**
