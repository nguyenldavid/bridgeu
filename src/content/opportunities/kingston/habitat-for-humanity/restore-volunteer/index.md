**Position description:**

- The Habitat for Humanity ReStore is a home and building supply store that accepts and sells donations of new & used building materials and furniture. The proceeds generated by the ReStore go directly to support Habitat for Humanity Kingston Limestone Region; where we strive to assist families build strength, stability and self-reliance through affordable homeownership.
- Habitat ReStore is always looking for volunteers to help with stocking shelves, picking up donations, and general store upkeep. Contact the ReStore Volunteer Coordinator to inquire about helping out!
