**Position description:**

- The Let's Talk Science Outreach program partners post-secondary and professional volunteers with teachers and community programs to lead children and youth through interactive STEM activities in the classroom or at special events. Virtual outreach opportunities are also available in some areas. Volunteers can also work to inspire youth across Canada to study STEM by sharing your expertise! Participate in virtual programming offered by the Let’s talk Science National Office, such as STEM Storytime for early years and career discovery events for older youth.
