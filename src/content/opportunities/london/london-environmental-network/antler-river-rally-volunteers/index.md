**Position description:**

- Antler River Rally is a volunteer group that organizes monthly clean-ups of the Thames River within the city of London, Ontario, Canada.
  
  We welcome new volunteers at our river clean-ups. We'll provide garbage bags and gloves (we also have a few garbage grabbers).
