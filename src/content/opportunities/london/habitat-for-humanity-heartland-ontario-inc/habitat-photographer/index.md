**Position description:**

- Photographers help capture designated photos of our Habitat Homeowners, our employees, volunteers and the community that we serve - on the build site and at any special event. Photographers must be able to take clear, engaging photos indoors and outdoors. The ideal candidate will have their own equipment and the ability to send photos electronically. If you’re looking to build your portfolio, expand your networking circle, or simply give back to your community, this position is for you.
