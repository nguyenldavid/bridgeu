**Position description:**

- We offer an experience like no other! We offer great hands-on opportunities for small groups, both on our build sites and at a Habitat ReStore. We provide tangible experiences that drive home the importance of community involvement.
- Our ReStores can accommodate between 5-15 team members and we will provide all equipment, onsite training and direction for each team member to make sure they feel comfortable and confident. Volunteers are essential to our ReStore success. Every volunteer that greets a customer, processes a donation or makes a sale keeps usable goods out of landfills and redirects funds to help Habitat homeowners build strength, stability and self- reliant futures. Multiply that impact by the efforts of your entire team and we are well on our way to a stronger, healthier community!
- If you have any further questions. Please contact the Volunteer Manager at 519-455-6623 Ext 207, or [kforrest@habitat4home.ca](mailto:kforrest@habitat4home.ca).
