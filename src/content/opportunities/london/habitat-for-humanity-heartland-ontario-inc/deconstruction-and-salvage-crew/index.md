**Position description:**

- Always interesting and never in the same place, this hands-on deconstruction team goes into various neighbourhoods to safely remove kitchens, doors, windows, bathrooms and more from corporations and individuals who are undertaking a renovation. The items are then brought back to resell in our ReStores. The ideal candidate should be knowledgeable in using power tools, and have transportation- as some areas are not accessible by bus. References are required for this positon.
