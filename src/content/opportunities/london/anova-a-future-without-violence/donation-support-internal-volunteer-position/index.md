**Position Description:**

- Responsibilities include: ensuring safe, clean, and good quality donations are available for residents, following all donation sorting protocols & practices, sorting donations brought in from community members, assisting the inventory coordinator with organizing and distributing donations, and clearing out old stock and re-stocking storage rooms as needed.
