**Position description:**

- Support Line Volunteers provide supportive, phone- based listening services to individuals across the Thames Valley region. In the event that a caller is in crisis, the volunteer will connect the caller with appropriate crisis or Emergency services. Individuals will volunteer for a minimum of 12 consecutive months, providing 11 hours per month (3 hours to be completed overnight between 12am-6am).
