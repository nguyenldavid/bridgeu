The Museum of Health Care is located in Kingston, Ontario, Canada in the Ann Baillie Building on the Kingston General Hospital site and covers medical history from the 18th century to the modern era. The story of Canadian health and medicine tells how people have preserved health and managed disease, pain, and suffering.
