Cycle Kingston uses volunteer power to manage its operations, and to deliver many of its cycling safety programs, especially in the off-season when we don't have summer staff. If you want to help us out - for a couple hours a month, or more - fill out the online form to the right, or send us an mailto:info@cyclekingston.ca
 to tell us what you're interested in doing. Thank you!
