LDAK is a charitable volunteer support group in Kingston, Ontario for people with learning disabilities, their families, and those who work with or employ persons with learning disabilities. We provide information, support and resources for interested community members.
 
LDAK is affiliated with the Learning Disabilities Association of Ontario and the Learning Disabilities Association of Canada.
