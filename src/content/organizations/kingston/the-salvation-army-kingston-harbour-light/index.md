The Salvation Army Kingston Harbour Light Residential Treatment Program has operated in Kingston since 1986, serving men from across Ontario who struggle with substance use disorders and concurrent mental health issues.
Our Program addresses recovery from addiction and concurrent disorders from a holistic perspective, addressing the physical, emotional, psychological and spiritual concerns of our clients.
The Salvation Army is grateful for the support of The South East Local Health Integration Network.
