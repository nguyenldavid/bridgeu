For Diverse Artists and Audiences: To inspire, stimulate, and transform our audiences through outstanding cultural and intercultural experiences and dialogue.
For Students: We and our academic partners at the DAN School of Drama and Music and the Department of Film and Media offer transformative student experiences through the intersections of multiple disciplines of education and the performing and film arts to develop them to their fullest potential as artists, pacesetters, and global citizens.
For Emerging Talent: We are an accelerator for the artistic development of emerging talent.
For Arts Creators: We are an incubator for new technologies and multi-disciplinary work creation.
