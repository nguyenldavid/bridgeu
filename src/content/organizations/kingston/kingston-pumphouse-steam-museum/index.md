There are lots of ways to volunteer with the PumpHouse, see our volunteer positions described below:
• Model Railway Volunteer: Operate, interpret, maintain, and improve the model electric railway layouts located in the museum Train Room.
• Research Volunteer: Research topics related to the PumpHouse as directed by a Curator.
