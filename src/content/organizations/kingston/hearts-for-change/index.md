Heart for Change’s mission is to help improve the quality of life to children living in orphanages or children’s homes in the most needy communities throughout the world by partnering with local organizations and assisting in providing the necessities of life for the children and contributing in creating a loving and nourishing environment in which they can live and grow to be successful members of society.
