Welcome to Dress for Success Kingston. We are dedicated to providing support, guidance, and mentorship to women in need in the Kingston and area community. We are part of a global movement for change and empowerment of women to achieve economic independence by providing a network of support, professional attire and the development tools to help women thrive in work and in life.
