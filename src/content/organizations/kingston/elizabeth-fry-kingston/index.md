Volunteer
Since 1949, Elizabeth Fry Kingston has been involved with women who are at risk of coming into conflict with the law. Volunteers, and the tremendous support they provide, are an integral part of the work we do. As a volunteer for Elizabeth Fry Kingston, you will gain valuable experience and insight and make a significant difference in the lives of the women we work with.
Our volunteer opportunities include:
• Office Administration 
• Court Accompaniment, Referral and Education (C.A.R.E.) Program 
• Detweiler House (Reliability Status clearance required) 
• Women Empowered (WE) Hub Programs 
• Community Kitchen and Garden 
• Board Member
