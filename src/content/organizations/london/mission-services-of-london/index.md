Mission Services of London is a non-profit organization dedicated to helping individuals and families facing poverty, homelessness, crisis, addiction, and mental illness. They offer safe shelter, nutritious food, clothing, outreach, counseling, and support services through five branches, including the Men's Mission & Rehabilitation Centre, Community Mental Health Programs, Rotholme Women's & Family Shelter, Quintin Warner House, and The Mission Store.
