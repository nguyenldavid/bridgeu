Compassion's ministry centers around "holistic child development," focusing on nurturing children in all aspects of life. They go beyond addressing poverty by providing age-appropriate Christian teaching and discipleship through local churches, offering formal and non-formal educational opportunities, delivering healthcare, hygiene training, and supplementary food, and ensuring that each child receives personal attention, guidance, and love. Through partnerships with local churches, Compassion aims to create a comprehensive support system that fosters the development of children in mind, body, and spirit.
