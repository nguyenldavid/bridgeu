PHSS is a non-profit organization that provides unique and flexible support and services so that people with complex medical, physical, and/or developmental needs can live the life they choose as part of the community. PHSS supports more than 250 individuals in more than 60 locations across Ontario, providing 24-hour residential support, day and overnight respite, specialized complex medical care and intensive behavioural support, recreational programs, and support for individuals living at home with their families.
