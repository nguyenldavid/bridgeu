The London Environmental Network's mission is to promote environmental protection and sustainability within the community. They achieve this by aiding local environmental organizations in enhancing their capacity and mission fulfillment. By offering shared resources, training, outreach, and collaboration opportunities, they enable these organizations to become more robust and work together more effectively. Additionally, they connect groups with the London community, assist in volunteer recruitment, and promote environmental events and initiatives.
