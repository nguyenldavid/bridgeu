SALT Rescue is like many other organizations, striving to reduce pet overpopulation by providing medical care, nutrition, and loving forever homes for animals in need. They also educate the public on the importance of spaying/neutering, vaccinations, and microchipping. SALT Rescue is committed to making a difference in every animal's life they encounter.
