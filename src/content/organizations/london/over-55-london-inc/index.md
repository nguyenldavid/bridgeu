We're London's sole organization committed to assisting mature entrepreneurs and promoting safe aging in place for seniors. We empower older adults on their entrepreneurial journey while providing essential services for them to age safely at home. Our approach involves helping older adults start micro-businesses utilizing their existing skills, resources, and our Skills At Work program. By creating job opportunities for seniors marginalized in the labor market, we support them in aging in place and offer subsidized services to seniors and those facing disabilities or barriers.
