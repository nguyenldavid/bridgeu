Homes for Hope is a non-profit, non-denominational organization focused on enhancing the lives of families in El Salvador. With the help of volunteers and donations from local Canadians, they create safe and secure housing, fostering hope, community, and lasting economic change. Their mission is to provide hope and improve the quality of life without discrimination based on any protected characteristic.
