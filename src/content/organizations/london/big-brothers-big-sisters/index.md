Big Brothers Big Sisters of London and Area is dedicated to improving youth health and well-being by intervening early in the lives of children facing adversity. Through their mentoring programs, they match children (Littles) with caring adult volunteers (Bigs). These mentorship relationships, fostered through weekly visits, enhance the self-esteem of Littles, strengthen their community connections, and inspire them to dream big, breaking the cycle of adverse childhood experiences.
