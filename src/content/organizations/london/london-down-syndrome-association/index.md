Established in 1983, the London Down Syndrome Association is a volunteer-run charity, raising funds to provide responsive, compassionate support services to children, youth, adults and families living with Down Syndrome in London, Ontario, and surrounding areas. Providing support within the counties of Elgin, Huron, Middlesex, Oxford and Perth.
