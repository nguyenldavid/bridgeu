Help for Headaches is a registered Canadian charity based in London, Ontario, with a mission to educate and empower headache and migraine sufferers in Ontario and beyond. They focus on various aspects, including education about headaches and migraines, non-drug treatment options, lifestyle changes, record-keeping, and staying updated on emerging approaches to alleviate these conditions.
