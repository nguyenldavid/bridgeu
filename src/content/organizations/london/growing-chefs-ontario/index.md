Our mission is to transform the way children, youth, families, and the community perceive and cultivate healthy relationships with food. We envision creating a robust, empowered community actively engaged with our food system. Our goals are to facilitate chefs and growers' community involvement in promoting food education, empower children with confidence, knowledge, and enthusiasm to cultivate and prepare nutritious cuisine, and promote the development of food education for children and families.
