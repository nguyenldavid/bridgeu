St. Leonard's Society of London, known as St. Leonard's Community Services, London & Region (SLCS), operates community-based support programs and residential centers. Their initiatives include assisting individuals in conflict or at risk with the justice system using restorative practices, advocating for improved justice system policies and service delivery, encouraging responsibility and accountability to address criminal behavior, and educating communities on their role in crime prevention and response.
