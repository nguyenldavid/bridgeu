Goodwill Industries is a non-profit social enterprise dedicated to providing work opportunities, skills development, and family strengthening for individuals facing barriers like disability or social disadvantage. Their goal is to promote economic self-sufficiency and prosperity for individuals, families, and communities. Goodwill creates jobs through various social enterprise platforms, unifying the values of care and business to unleash individual and community potential. Beyond employment, they offer benefits, life coaching, wellness, training, education access, career growth, poverty reduction, and social inclusion.
