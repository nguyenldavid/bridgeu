The Independent Living Centre of London and Area (ILCLA) is a charitable, not-for-profit organization dedicated to empowering people with disabilities through consumer-directed services and programs. They rely on volunteer support, fundraising, and grassroots donations. ILCLA offers various programs, including information and networking, peer support, independent living skills development, and community research. They host free social and educational activities, such as weekly exercise classes and outings, open to everyone.
