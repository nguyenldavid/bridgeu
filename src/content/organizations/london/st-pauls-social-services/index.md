St. Paul's Cathedral has been home to St. Paul's Social Services for more than three decades, generously supported by parishioners who volunteer their time, donate food, and provide financial assistance. The dedicated volunteers come from various faith communities, some with over 25 years of service, including high school students and those from Western University and Fanshawe College. St. Paul's Social Services is an ecumenical organization funded by individuals, faith communities, corporations, service clubs, and charitable foundations.
