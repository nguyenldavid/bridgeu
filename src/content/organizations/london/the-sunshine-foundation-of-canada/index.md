Children and youth in Canada living with severe physical disabilities face unrelenting challenges of social isolation, financial stress, and anxiety that has been further compounded by the effects of the pandemic.
Without Sunshine, many see themselves as having a disability first and societal contributor second. Our programs shift that paradigm and helps youth gain confidence, experience equity, diversity, and inclusion, and have a brighter future outlook.
