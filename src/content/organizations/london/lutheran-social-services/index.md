Lutheran Social Services is a Canadian charity dedicated to supporting children, youth, the elderly, and individuals facing illness or challenges. Their mission is to provide essential services, develop programs, and support research to enhance the quality of life and self-sufficiency for these groups. They value initiatives that prioritize the well-being of children and youth, striving to make a positive impact on their lives.
