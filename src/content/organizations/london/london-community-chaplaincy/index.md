London Community Chaplaincy supports families living in Southdale and Limberlost, two major low-income housing complexes in London. These families, often single parents or working poor, confront daily challenges associated with poverty, including mental health, addiction, abuse, food scarcity, and financial insecurity. LCC reaches out with love, offering support, tailored activities, and programs to address their needs. By being a presence in the community, LCC builds relationships, provides encouragement, and fosters opportunities for learning and growth in a safe, healthy environment.
