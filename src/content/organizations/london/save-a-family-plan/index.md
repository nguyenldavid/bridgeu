Save A Family Plan (SAFP) is a Canadian international non-governmental organization founded in 1965 by Monsignor Augustine Kandathil. Their mission is to promote justice and social change by supporting impoverished families and communities in India. SAFP implements programs that empower these families to become self-sufficient and contribute to the betterment of their communities.
