The Shrine was initially seen as a place for Masons to unwind and have fun, with activities like bands, parades, and costumes. Over time, its membership grew steadily. Since 1920, Mocha's primary charity has been Shriners' Hospitals for Children, and they've raised substantial funds for this cause. They acquired a home on Waterloo Street in London for meetings and functions. Mocha Temple is a welcoming place for Shriners to bring their families and friends for lunch.
