The Muslim Resource Centre for Social Support and Integration (MRCSSI) is a non-profit, charitable agency in London dedicated to promoting family safety and well-being within the diverse Muslim communities. MRCSSI uses a Culturally Integrative Model that considers migration, integration, culture, and values to provide evidence-based services tailored to families and individuals from collectivist cultures, those facing integration challenges, and those migrating from conflict zones. Their approach is family-focused, strengths-based, and collective in nature, embedded within cultural norms.
