The USC Foundation is a registered Canadian charitable foundation 
focused primarily on promoting the health and well-being of Western University students by providing them with physical and mental health support services. The Foundation is also invested in addressing and preventing problems faced by youth, in particular, youth unemployment, by providing job skills training and employment assistance to students.
