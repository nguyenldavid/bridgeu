As a Christian Organization, The Salvation Army Centre of Hope responds to the emotional, spiritual and physical needs of those within our sphere of influence, leading to self-healing and regeneration through supportive, multi-faceted programs. The Salvation Army Centre of Hope is committed to excellence, empowering those within our sphere of influence to achieve their own new beginning. Hope can come in many ways to us. Here at the Centre of Hope we have built faith and hope into all we do, from our services to men, women, and youth who are absolutely homeless to the families we serve in our community who are at risk of homelessness.
