Hutton House Association for Adults with Disabilities is a registered charity with over 65 years experience in facilitating the goals and aspirations of adults with disabilities. Within each program offered, individuals may customize their involvement to maximize their own capacity to learn, work, live and participate in the community. In turn, our vision will be realized by building community partnerships and opportunities that strengthen the inclusion of all citizens in society. Hutton House provides opportunities for education, art, enrichment, fitness, etc. for our members.
