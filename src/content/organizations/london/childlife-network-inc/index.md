Our role is to help the most vulnerable through comprehensive programs designed to mentor and educate the children of Uganda. Our goal, through our curriculum, is to help provide a springboard out of poverty and ultimately a better way of life. The Children’s Hope Center is our base of operations and resource center, located in Kabalagala, Kampala, Uganda. In addition to hosting our three main programs, the Children’s Hope Center also runs various aft er school programs that offer children the chance to play recreational sports, get help with homework, and learn how to use a computer. Childlife also runs additional educational and professional training programs in Uganda.
