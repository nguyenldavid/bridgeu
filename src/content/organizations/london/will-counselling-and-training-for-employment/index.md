For over 37 years, WILL Employment Solutions has connected immigrants and employers across Ontario, offering specialized services tailored to individual needs. Whether you're a newcomer starting over, an immigrant in a regulated profession looking to restart your career, or an employer seeking to recruit international talent, WILL Employment Solutions provides welcoming, innovative solutions for continuous learning. As the exclusive full-service, no-cost immigrant employment solutions provider in Ontario, they are committed to benefiting both immigrants and employers.
