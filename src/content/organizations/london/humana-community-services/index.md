We unite over a century of experience in providing high-quality services and support to individuals with complex needs, as well as their families and caregivers. Our services encompass crisis support, personalized mental health assistance, aid for community involvement and transitioning to independent living, and the provision of safe and therapeutic educational environments for at-risk children, youth, and vulnerable adults. Humana Community Services remains committed to helping individuals facing diverse challenges, including trauma, mental health issues, developmental hurdles, unstable housing, homelessness, and legal conflicts. We aim to strengthen our programs, enhance coordination, and introduce family navigation services to better address evolving needs.
