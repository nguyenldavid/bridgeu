Sewing Seeds is a Canadian charity rooted in faith, dedicated to empowering individuals economically through sewing skills training. Comprised of industry professionals, they prioritize excellence, quality, sustainability, and social responsibility in their programs. Sewing Seeds is passionate about fostering unity, cultural respect, and building confidence in individuals. They provide support, hope, and encouragement for students and volunteers to reach their full potential.
