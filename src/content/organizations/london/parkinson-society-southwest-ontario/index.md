Parkinson's Society Southwestern Ontario is a supportive home for all individuals living with Parkinson's Disease in the Southwestern Ontario region. They offer various services, workshops, and groups to foster a community dedicated to Parkinson's care. Their focus spans education, advocacy, and research initiatives aimed at enhancing awareness and support for Parkinson's Disease. The organization emphasizes togetherness and prides itself on a dedicated team of staff providing essential resources and unwavering care to improve the lives of those affected by Parkinson's in Southwestern Ontario.
