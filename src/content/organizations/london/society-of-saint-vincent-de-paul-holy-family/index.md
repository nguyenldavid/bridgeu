For over 50 years, the SSVP Conference at Holy Family Parish has been dedicated to assisting the less fortunate in the north-west section of London. Their mission is to embody the Gospel message by serving those in need with love, respect, justice, and joy. They provide emergency food assistance, home visits, clothing vouchers, help with educational bursaries, and support various programs while following their founders' examples of humility and respect in serving the less fortunate, sharing the love of God with all.
