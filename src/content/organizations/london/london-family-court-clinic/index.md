The London Family Court Clinic, now known as the Centre for Children & Families in the Justice System, is dedicated to providing essential services for individuals and families involved in the justice system. With a staff of 25 and expertise in 13 different service areas, their mission is to offer a wide range of services, including assessments, court testimony, alternative dispute resolution, and prevention and intervention resources. They are committed to supporting children and families within the justice system to ensure their well-being and provide guidance during challenging times.
