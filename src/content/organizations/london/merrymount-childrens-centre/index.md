Merrymount's mission is to assist children and families during and after crises, fostering stronger, healthier family units. Their safe, supportive, and strength-based approach ensures children have the necessary support to develop resilience, build healthy relationships, and feel valued. Since 1874, Merrymount has provided these unique services to London's families, being the only center of its kind in Canada. They are a dedicated organization, offering round-the-clock support and crisis care to empower children and families during times of change.
