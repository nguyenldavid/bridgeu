At The Angel Project we not only raise funds to assist in at least one outing per eligible patient per year, when possible we assist with the purchase of items such as wheelchairs, voice boxes and speech computers. We also assist with basic needs such as providing soap, shaving cream, shampoo and even simple comforts such as stuffed animals, soft blankets or gifts at Christmas.
