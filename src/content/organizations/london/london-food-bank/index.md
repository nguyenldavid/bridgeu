Established in 1986, the London Food Bank, led by co-directors Jane Roy and Glen Pearson, remains a thriving, volunteer-driven, and transparent community organization even after 35 years. Thanks to dedicated volunteers, staff, and consistent donations, the Food Bank continues to serve the city. Their mission is to facilitate the sharing of food resources within the community, with a dual mandate: to assist those facing financial hardship directly and to collaborate with other agencies helping people in need as a food warehouse.
