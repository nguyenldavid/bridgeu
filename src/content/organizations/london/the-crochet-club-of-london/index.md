The Crochet Club of London, established in 2002, is a volunteer-driven, non-denominational organization dedicated to creating blankets for charitable organizations in the London community. With approximately 130 members of all ages and abilities, the club's mission is to provide warmth and comfort through the donation of blankets to local charities. They have donated over 16,000 blankets to local charitable organizations, including pet blankets for animal shelters, and have contributed more than 450 silent auction blankets to support fundraising activities. The organization's values center around community impact, volunteerism, and making a difference in the lives of those in need.
